About Us

We are a multi-language web document translator application with the intention of helping people get acess to documents from one lanaguage they arent familiar with to the one they are. Our goal started with the ideas to help people translatre research documents to closen the gap in multi-language research. Our goal in the future is to integrate a chatbot that will help translating the documents but also clue in on the parts the user are havign questions about. This is an application that was made for people who are students, professors, and the common person to use.
